About Fridge Filter Depot

Fridge Filter Depot sells replacement water filters for Samsung, LG, Whirlpool and GE refrigerators. Orders ship from our U.S. warehouse.

We built this store to make one thing easy: finding the right filter for your refrigerator. Search by model number or part number, and if you are not sure, send us a photo of your old filter. We will confirm the fit before you order.
