Refrigerator Water Filter FAQ

How do I find the right water filter for my refrigerator?

Use the model number on the label inside your refrigerator or the part number on your old filter. Search it in our finder, or message us a photo on WhatsApp and we will confirm it before you order.

How often should I replace a refrigerator water filter?

Most manufacturers recommend about every six months, or sooner if the water flow slows down or the taste changes.

Do you ship from the United States?

What if I order the wrong filter?

Send us your refrigerator model number or a photo of the old filter before you order and we will confirm the fit. See our shipping and returns page for the return policy.

Can I change my refrigerator water filter after 12 months?

It will still pass water, but a carbon filter that old is likely saturated and can no longer reduce chlorine taste or odor as intended. Most manufacturers recommend replacing it about every six months.

Is the filter model the same as the refrigerator model?

No. The refrigerator has a model number and the filter has its own part number (for example DA29-00020B or LT700P). One filter part number fits many refrigerator models.

Do I need to turn off the water to change the filter?

Most internal refrigerator filters do not require shutting off the water. Follow your manual; some models ask you to turn the water supply valve off first.

How many gallons should I flush?

It depends on the brand and model. Many guides ask for about 2 to 4 gallons of water, but check your owner's manual.

Why is my water filter light still on after replacing the filter?

The reset may not have registered, or the filter may not be fully seated. Press and hold the reset button again and check that the filter is locked in.

How do I know which Samsung filter I need?

Use the model number inside your refrigerator or the part number printed on the old filter. If you are unsure, send us a photo on WhatsApp.
