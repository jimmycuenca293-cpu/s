Samsung filter FAQ

How do I know which Samsung water filter I need?

Use the model number on the label inside the refrigerator or the part number on your old filter, and compare it with the list on this page. If you are unsure, message us a photo.

How often should I replace a Samsung refrigerator water filter?

About every six months, or sooner if the water flow slows or the taste changes.

How do I reset the Samsung filter light?

Press and hold the Water Filter button (or the Alarm / Filter Reset button, depending on the control panel) for about 3 seconds until the filter light turns off.
