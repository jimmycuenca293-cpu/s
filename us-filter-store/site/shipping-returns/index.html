Shipping and Returns

Shipping

Orders ship from our U.S. warehouse. We send tracking information by email when your order ships. Delivery to your door is included in the price of the filters shown with a price; if shipping is charged, it is shown at checkout.

Returns

If you ordered the wrong filter, contact us before you open the package. Unopened filters in original packaging can be returned; contact us on WhatsApp to start a return. Opened or used filters cannot be returned for hygiene reasons unless they are defective.

Damaged or defective items

If your order arrives damaged or defective, send us a photo and your order number and we will make it right.

Policy details may be updated. Contact us with any questions before ordering.
