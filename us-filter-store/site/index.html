How to find the right refrigerator water filter

  1. Find your model number. It is on a label inside the refrigerator, on a side wall or near the crisper drawers. Where to look.
  2. Match it to a filter. Search your model or the part number printed on the old filter in our filter finder or the part number cross-reference.
  3. Order and replace. Pay with PayPal, and replace the filter about every six months. How to change it.

Why replace your refrigerator water filter?

A refrigerator water filter traps particles and reduces chlorine taste and odor, so the water and ice from your dispenser taste cleaner. The filter has a limited capacity: after about six months of normal use it is saturated and water can taste or flow differently. Most refrigerators remind you with a filter light.

Replacing the filter on time is the simplest way to keep water and ice tasting fresh. Read how often to change it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the right water filter for my refrigerator?

Use the model number on the label inside your refrigerator or the part number on your old filter. Search it in our finder, or message us a photo on WhatsApp and we will confirm it before you order.

How often should I replace a refrigerator water filter?

Most manufacturers recommend about every six months, or sooner if the water flow slows down or the taste changes.
