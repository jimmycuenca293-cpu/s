About the Samsung HAF-EX external (DA29-10105J)

The Samsung HAF-EX external DA29-10105J Refrigerator Water Filter is an external inline water filter that connects to the water line outside the refrigerator and filters the water that feeds the dispenser and the ice maker. It is a replacement for the Samsung HAF-EX external, part number DA29-10105J.

An external inline filter: it mounts on the water line outside the refrigerator, so it also suits dispenser models that do not have an internal filter.

Replace your refrigerator water filter about every six months, or sooner if the water flow slows down or the taste changes.

How to replace it

  1. Turn off the water supply to the refrigerator and relieve the pressure by running the dispenser for a moment.
  2. Mount the filter on the water line, following the arrow that shows the direction of water flow.
  3. Connect the tubing to the inlet and outlet fittings and make sure they are fully seated.
  4. Turn the water back on slowly and check every connection for leaks.
  5. Flush the filter by running water through the dispenser until it runs clear and steady.
