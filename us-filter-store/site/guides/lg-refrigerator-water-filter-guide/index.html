LG refrigerator water filters: which one do you need?

LG uses several different water filter designs, and a filter that looks similar is not always the right one. The quickest way to choose correctly is to start from your refrigerator model number or the part number on your old filter.

Start with the model number

Find the model number on the label inside the refrigerator (see our guide on where it is). Then check the compatibility list on each LG filter page, or use the finder.

Match the part number

Each filter below lists its main part number and the other numbers it is also known by. If your old filter or manual shows any of those, you are in the right place.

Internal or external filter?

Most LG refrigerators use an internal cartridge. An external inline filter is an alternative for some dispenser models that do not have an internal filter.

Replacing and resetting

LG filters are usually replaced about every six months. Press and hold the Water Filter button (or Filter Reset) for about 3 seconds until the indicator light turns off or the display confirms the reset.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know which LG filter I need?

Use the model number inside your refrigerator or the part number printed on the old filter. If you are unsure, send us a photo on WhatsApp.

How often should I change my LG water filter?

About every six months, or sooner if water flow slows or the taste changes.

Do all LG refrigerators use the same filter?

No. Different models use different filters, so always confirm with your model number.
