Can you use a refrigerator without a water filter?

Yes, in most refrigerators you can, as long as the filter slot is not left open. Water will not flow without either a filter or a bypass plug.

What happens

  • The dispenser and ice maker will work normally with a bypass plug installed.
  • Water and ice are not filtered, so taste and odor may be less pleasant, and sediment can reach the ice maker.

How to do it

Use the bypass plug supplied by the manufacturer for your model. If you do not have one, order the correct plug or filter for your refrigerator.

Keep in mind

Do not leave the filter slot empty: water will not flow and some models will leak.
