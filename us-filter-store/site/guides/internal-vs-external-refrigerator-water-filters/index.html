Internal vs external refrigerator water filters

Most refrigerators use an internal cartridge, but an external inline filter on the water line is another way to filter water for the dispenser and ice maker.

Internal cartridge

Sits inside the refrigerator in a dedicated housing. It is designed for your model and is easy to change: twist or push, remove and replace.

External inline filter

Mounts on the water line outside the refrigerator. It is useful for dispenser models without an internal filter housing and for ice makers. It is a bit more involved to install because you connect it to the water line.

Which to choose

If your refrigerator has an internal filter housing, use the filter designed for it. Choose an external filter when there is no internal option or when you want to filter the supply to the ice maker.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use both?

Some people do, but check your refrigerator's manual and the filters' instructions.

Do external filters need a tool to install?

Usually they connect with push-in fittings or compression fittings. Follow the filter's instructions.
