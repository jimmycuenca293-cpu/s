Refrigerator water filter leaking: what to check

A leak around the filter is a sign that something is not sealing. Stop using the dispenser until you find out why.

Possible causes

  • The filter is not fully inserted or turned to the locked position.
  • An O-ring is missing, twisted or damaged. Check the new filter before installing it.
  • The filter is not the correct part number for the model.
  • The housing is cracked or the water line fitting is loose.
  • The filter was cross-threaded when installed.

What to do

  • Turn off the water supply, remove the filter and inspect the O-rings and the housing.
  • Reinstall the filter, aligning it carefully and turning only until it locks. Do not over-tighten.
  • If it still leaks, the part may not be the right one for your refrigerator: confirm the part number or message us a photo.

Frequently asked questions

Is a small drip normal?

A little water when you remove the old filter is normal. A continuing leak after installation is not.

Can I use Teflon tape on the filter?

No. Refrigerator filters seal with O-rings, not thread tape.
