Cloudy ice or smelly ice from the refrigerator

Ice absorbs odors easily, and it holds the taste of whatever water and air it was made with. A worn-out filter is a common cause.

Possible causes

  • The water filter is overdue for replacement.
  • Old ice has been sitting in the bin for a long time.
  • Strong-smelling food in the freezer, especially if it is uncovered.
  • Mineral content in the water: cloudy ice can come from dissolved air and minerals.

What to do

  • Replace the filter and flush it.
  • Empty the ice bin, wash it and let the ice maker make fresh ice.
  • Keep foods covered in the freezer.

Frequently asked questions

Is cloudy ice a sign the filter is bad?

Not always. Cloudy ice can be due to air and minerals. A bad taste or odor is more likely connected to the filter.

How often should I clean the ice bin?

Every few months, or whenever the ice has an off taste.
