LG filter FAQ

How do I know which LG water filter I need?

Use the model number on the label inside the refrigerator or the part number on your old filter, and compare it with the list on this page. If you are unsure, message us a photo.

How often should I replace a LG refrigerator water filter?

About every six months, or sooner if the water flow slows or the taste changes.

How do I reset the LG filter light?

Press and hold the Water Filter button (or Filter Reset) for about 3 seconds until the indicator light turns off or the display confirms the reset.
