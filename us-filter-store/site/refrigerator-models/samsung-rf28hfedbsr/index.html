How to change the RF28HFEDBSR water filter

  1. Find the old filter in your refrigerator (usually inside the fresh-food compartment or in the base grille) and open the cover if there is one.
  2. Turn the old filter counterclockwise, or press the release button, and pull it straight out.
  3. Remove the protective cap from the new filter and check that the O-rings are in place.
  4. Insert the new filter and turn it clockwise until it locks.
  5. Reset the filter indicator light and run water through the dispenser to flush the new filter (see your manual for the amount).

Reset the filter light

Press and hold the Water Filter button (or the Alarm / Filter Reset button, depending on the control panel) for about 3 seconds until the filter light turns off. Your manual has the exact steps for your model.
