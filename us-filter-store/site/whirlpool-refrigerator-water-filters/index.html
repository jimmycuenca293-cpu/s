Whirlpool filter FAQ

How do I know which Whirlpool water filter I need?

Use the model number on the label inside the refrigerator or the part number on your old filter, and compare it with the list on this page. If you are unsure, message us a photo.

How often should I replace a Whirlpool refrigerator water filter?

About every six months, or sooner if the water flow slows or the taste changes.

How do I reset the Whirlpool filter light?

Press and hold the Water Filter (or Reset Water Filter) button for about 3 seconds until the indicator light turns off. On some models the reset is in the touch display menu.
